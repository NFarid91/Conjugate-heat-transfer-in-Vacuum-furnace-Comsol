# Conjugate Heat Transfer in a Vacuum Furnace — COMSOL

## Overview
A multiphysics simulation project developed in COMSOL Multiphysics to study heat transfer and transient quenching behavior in a vacuum-furnace environment.

## Objectives
- Model heat transfer between the furnace environment and the workpiece.
- Investigate transient temperature evolution during quenching.
- Study the coupled thermal and fluid-thermal behavior of the system.
- Demonstrate the application of COMSOL for multiphysics heat-transfer analysis.

## Software
- COMSOL Multiphysics

## Physics / Engineering Topics
- Conjugate heat transfer (CHT)
- Transient heat transfer
- Fluid flow and heat transfer
- Vacuum-furnace thermal processes
- Quenching / cooling analysis
- Temperature-field evaluation

## Repository Contents
The repository contains simulation-related media and recorded results illustrating the transient quenching process and different stages of the simulation.

## Engineering Applications
The methodology is relevant to thermal processing equipment, heat-treatment systems, furnace design, and the analysis of coupled fluid and solid heat transfer.

## Notes
The repository can be expanded with the COMSOL model file, geometry, boundary conditions, material properties, solver settings, mesh information, and quantitative results to make the study fully reproducible.
